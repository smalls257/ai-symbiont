1. What the judge leaves believing

Frontier models serve everyone. A small personal learner, trained on your own agent history, makes them yours.
For company teams that have session history, and still runnable by a solo dev on one box (Glimmer on the Strix). The dev installs one thing (MCP server + hooks), picks projects in a guided setup, and then sees warnings and recalls appear inline in chat. hypothesis: that this helps in practice is what Act 2 measures
